About SPM

Focused by structure. Selective by design.

Secure Projects Management LLC was established in Wyoming in 2021 as a privately held company for the management of proprietary capital and selected investment activities.

SPM operates for its own account. Its approach is centred on careful evaluation, disciplined oversight and the selective deployment of company capital where opportunity, structure and risk are considered appropriately aligned.

Our Approach

Four principles guide our decisions.

Selectivity

We are not compelled to deploy capital. Opportunities must merit consideration.

Discipline

Decisions are approached through structured evaluation rather than short-term sentiment.

Oversight

Activities are monitored within an internal governance and risk framework.

Alignment

Capital deployment is assessed against SPM's own objectives and risk parameters.

Governance

Structure, oversight and accountability.

Capital preservation and disciplined decision-making sit at the centre of our approach.

SPM maintains corporate governance and compliance processes appropriate to its activities and structure. Investment decisions are undertaken for the company's own account and are subject to internal review, risk assessment and applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
